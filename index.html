Where the numbers come from

Calories per cup and per kg are copied from each maker's own product page (metabolizable energy as fed), with a link to the page and the date checked. Makers revise formulas; check the bag in your hands. Dry formulas only; this page lists from .

Daily energy: resting energy RER = 70 × (kg)0.75, times a life-stage factor from Table 1 of the Ohio State University Veterinary Medical Center calorie calculator: neutered adult 1.6, intact adult 1.8, inactive or obesity-prone 1.2–1.4 (we use 1.3), puppy under 4 months 3.0, puppy 4 months to adult 2.0. These are starting points for healthy dogs. Dogs with a health condition, working dogs and dogs that need to lose weight should follow their vet's number. Adjust by ±10% to keep your dog's body condition steady. Tufts' Petfoodology explains why a cup of one food is not a cup of another.
